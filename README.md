# FS25_MarketsAndFutures
FS25_MarketsAndFutures

Takes control of every sell price in the game and lets you lock in prices with contracts.

- Prices drift hour by hour and react to random SURPLUS (price drop) and DEMAND (price spike) events for each crop and product. Related crops move together.
- Events last 3 to 14 game months. Everything is counted in months, so the market feels the same at any days-per-month setting.
- Contracts: choose an item, an amount (1,000 - 500,000 L) and a duration in game months (1 - 120). The locked price is the contract station's price, adjusted by the item's contract rate (-25% to +25%) and a contract fee. Deliver to that station before the deadline. Missing the deadline or cancelling early costs a penalty, and delivering enough earns a free cancel.
- A large, mouse-friendly menu: market list with Price Median, Station Average and Best Station columns, a station list with distances, events, history, price alerts and settings.
- Block Stations tab and an automatic access check keep unusable stations out of prices and contracts.
- A rotating on-screen HUD, 13 colour themes, rounded panels and item icons.
- Your HUD items, price alerts and hidden Stored Items are saved separately for each savegame.
- Works in singleplayer and multiplayer (the host controls the market settings).
- Price alerts watch the best station price. A triggered alert shows a pulsing banner on the HUD (with a sound you can turn off) until you clear it or the price no longer meets it.
- Move items between categories and make up to 3 categories of your own, saved per player and per savegame.

Open the menu with LEFT CTRL + M (all keys can be changed in Settings > Controls; look for the "Markets and Futures" entries).

Detailed mod information can be found in the mod file (Markets and Futures - Mod Description)

## Download and install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/jhome16/FS25_MarketsAndFutures/releases/latest) and download **FS25_MarketsAndFutures.zip**.
   Direct link: https://github.com/jhome16/FS25_MarketsAndFutures/releases/latest/download/FS25_MarketsAndFutures.zip
2. Put the zip file, still zipped, into your mods folder:
   - Windows: `Documents\My Games\FarmingSimulator2025\mods`
   - Mac: `~/Library/Application Support/FarmingSimulator2025/mods`
3. Start the game and enable **Markets and Futures** in your savegame's mod list.

Do not use the green **Code > Download ZIP** button. That zip has the wrong layout and the game will not load it.
